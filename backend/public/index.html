How it works

Set up in minutes, not meetings

No lengthy onboarding — create or join a group, and Aurenda takes it from there.

01

Create or join a group

Start a group for whoever it actually is — family, roommates, a church group, a trip — and invite people with a code.

02

Add what matters

Events, shared lists, routines — added by anyone, visible to the whole group or just the people you choose.

03

Stay in sync automatically

Everyone sees changes as they happen, conflicts get flagged before they become a problem, and nothing falls through the cracks.

Features

Everything your group actually needs

No assumptions about who you are — just the coordination tools every small group needs, with the flexibility to fit yours.

Shared Calendar

Day, week, month, and year views. Filter by Personal, one specific group, or merge everything across every group you belong to.

Group Workspaces

Belong to more than one group? Switch between them from a single screen — each with its own members, lists, and calendar.

Agenda & Conflict Detection

See what's next at a glance, and get flagged automatically when two plans overlap so nothing gets double-booked.

Shared Lists

Flexible lists for shopping, tasks, or anything else — free-text, not locked to any one kind of errand.

Activity Tracking

Log and share activities with your group, with optional step-count sharing you control.

Reminders That Stand Out

Themed notification cards that match your group's look, not a generic system alert.
