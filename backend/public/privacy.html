Privacy Policy

Last updated September 7, 2026

Aurenda ("we", "our", "the app") is a coordination app for small groups — family, roommates, couples, church groups, travel crews, study groups, or any other circle of people. This page explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and how you can request it be deleted.

What we collect

  • Account details: the email address and password you sign up with. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never in plain text.
  • Display name, used to identify you to other members of your group(s).
  • Group data: the groups you create or join, your role in each, and the events, lists, activities, and plans you or your group members add.
  • Step count, read from Apple Health, only if you turn on step sharing yourself. This is read-only — Aurenda never writes to Health data.
  • Profile photo, if you add one. This is stored only on your device — it is never uploaded to our servers.
  • Local profiles you create for people without their own account (for example, a young child) are just a name and color you choose. They don't collect any personal data of their own.

How we use it

Your data is used to sync your calendar, lists, and activities across your own devices and with the other members of whichever group(s) you choose to share them with. Each item you create has a visibility setting — personal to you, shared with your whole group, or shared with specific people — and we honor that setting.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with third parties for marketing. We do not currently use any third-party analytics or tracking services inside the app.

Where it's stored

Group and account data is stored in a database we operate on Railway. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS); account sessions use signed tokens rather than storing your password on your device.

Your choices

  • You can leave a group at any time from within the app.
  • You can turn step sharing off at any time; this stops any future step data from being read.
  • You can remove your profile photo at any time from Settings — since it's stored only on your device, removing it deletes it completely.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account directly in the app, from Settings → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently removes your account. Any group only you belong to is deleted along with it; a group you share with others stays, with ownership passed to another member instead of being erased — similar to how a group chat isn't deleted when one person leaves it. If you'd rather have us do it for you, email dhamyc245@gmail.com from the address on your account.

Children

Aurenda is not directed at children under 13, and account creation is not intended for children. Local profiles (see above) let a parent or guardian represent a child within their own group without that child having any account or personal data of their own.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
